# Phase 2 validation protocol (T10), fixed 2026-10-11 BEFORE any T10 data was pulled or analysed

Everything below (windows, thresholds, controls, pass criteria) is fixed here. If a test fails it is reported as failed; no re-run with changed thresholds. Choices that depend on data that did not exist yet when this was written (e.g. how many reports survive) are reported, never used to change a definition. A sha256 of this file is printed at the top of `reports/phase2_validation.md`.

Labels: all results are **hindcast** (NWM v3.0 retrospective, free-running, no data assimilation), self-controlled, SCENARIO-level. None is a forecast skill or a closure probability.

## A. Report set
- Source: IEM Local Storm Reports (`mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/geojson/lsr.geojson`), state = NC, 2016-01-01 .. 2022-12-31 (inside the NWM retrospective window), all NC counties.
- Candidate = same rule as phase 0 `s3b_tiers.py`: closure wording AND road wording AND flood cause (type FLASH FLOOD/FLOOD or flood words), AND NOT tree/power-line wording (`TREE|POWER ?LINE|DOWNED LINE|LINES DOWN|POLE`), AND NOT surge/tide wording (`SURGE|TIDE|TIDAL|OVERWASH`), AND remark does not name a different state. Duplicate (same time, remark, lat, lon) removed.
- Crossing set: NCDOT structure points (bridge/culvert/pipe) + NBI NC 2025 bridges, waterway-like feature names (phase-0 `WATER` regex, same exclusions), NCDOT/NBI merged spatially (<= 50 m), statewide. Each crossing matched to the nearest NWM reach <= 100 m (blank otherwise), with recurrence flows from NOAA OWP `static_nwm_flowlines` (q2, q5, q10, q25, q50). Not assessed: no reach, or q2 < 1 cfs.
- A report is **accepted-candidate** when a crossing from that set, assessed, sits on a road whose carried name/ref is named in the remark (phase-0 `mentions` logic, cross-street landmark matches are not excluded automatically but flagged) within **500 m** of the report point (coordinates rounded to ~1 km by NWS). Nearest such crossing is the report's crossing.
- All candidates are written to a review CSV with a suggested decision. **Nothing is auto-accepted.** Until the user fills `reviewer_decision`, the analysis runs on my suggested-accept rows and is labelled "provisional (unreviewed)". Where `reviewer_decision` is non-blank it overrides.
- Storm clusters (for bootstrap): sort accepted reports by time; a gap of more than **48 h** starts a new storm cluster (statewide).

## B. Flow percentile test (primary)
- Report hour h = report time floored to the hour (UTC). Primary statistic: NWM retro streamflow of the crossing's reach at hour h.
- Reference record: that reach's full hourly record in the retrospective, **1979-02-01 01:00 .. 2023-01-31 23:00 UTC** (all hours; missing stays NaN and is excluded).
- Percentile P = (share of reference hours strictly below the value) + half the share equal. Ratios: flow/q2, flow/q10 (OWP values, fixed in advance as the only thresholds; q5/q25/q50 are not used).
- Controls: for each report, **200** random hours on the same reach, same calendar month (any year in the reference record), excluding every hour within +-72 h of any accepted report on that reach. RNG seed 20261011.
- Reported: median P of reports vs of pooled controls; share above q2 and above q10 for reports vs controls; **AUC** = mean over reports of P(report flow > control flow) + 0.5 P(tie) within that report's own controls (matched AUC); 95% cluster-bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples of storm clusters, percentile method).
- **Pass:** AUC lower 95% bound > 0.5 AND (share of reports above q2 - share of controls above q2) lower 95% bound > 0. Otherwise the layer is reported as not shown to discriminate report times.
- Pre-declared secondaries (do not change the verdict): window-max flow over h-6..h+6 instead of the single hour; reaches of order >= 2 only; leave-one-storm-cluster-out AUC for clusters with >= 5 reports; AUC against the share of controls above q10.

## C. Timing test
- For each report and each control hour, take the window [t-72 h, t+72 h]; t_peak = hour of maximum flow (earliest on ties); timing hit = |t_peak - t| <= **24 h**.
- Reported: hit share for reports vs controls (the control hit share is the chance level for this window), 95% cluster-bootstrap CI of the difference. Also the same restricted to windows whose max exceeds q2.
- **Pass:** lower 95% bound of the difference (all windows) > 0.

## D. Gauge validation (T9, 43 pilot gauges)
- Gauges: the pilot-flag gauges in `data/derived/gauges_all.csv` with a matched NWM reach and observed hourly discharge in `obs_hourly.parquet`.
- Events: Matthew 2016-10-05..2016-10-25 and Florence 2018-09-10..2018-10-31 (the two pilot storms). A gauge-event is scored when observed discharge has >= 80% coverage in the window.
- Metrics per gauge-event: NSE and KGE of NWM retro vs observed discharge (hourly), peak ratio (NWM peak / observed peak), peak timing error (hours, NWM minus observed), and for exceedance of the reach's q2 (OWP): hours both above, hours only observed above, hours only NWM above.
- **Pass (declared in advance):** at least 70% of scored gauge-events have a peak ratio between 0.5 and 2.0, AND the median absolute peak timing error is <= 12 h. NSE/KGE and exceedance agreement are reported but are not part of the verdict.

## E. Documented closures (Florence, 2018-09-14 .. 09-30)
- `docs/florence_documented_closures.csv`: only closures with a retrievable source URL and a quote; time or section not stated -> `unverified`. Nothing guessed.
- Model "closed" at hour t on a crossing if hindcast flow > RL10 (q10). RL2 and RL50 are sensitivities. Crossing set = statewide seed crossings on the documented road in the documented county.
- Per verified closure with a stated start time and at least one assessed crossing on that road in that county: **hit** = at least one such crossing is model-closed for >= 1 h inside [closed, reopened]; **timing hit** = the first model-closed hour is within +-24 h of the documented closing. Contrast: model-closed share of hours in the 14 days before 2018-09-10 (control period) for the same crossings.
- Rows with no assessed crossing on that road/county are "not testable", not misses.
- **Exit-loss comparison:** the existing phase-1b exit-loss timeline (RL10 and RL2, seed) compared with documented closure times of the exit roads. Described as matches/misses per exit road; no pass/fail number.
- **Pass (declared in advance):** hit share among testable closures >= 60% AND timing-hit share >= 40%.

## F. Routing validation
- 150 origin blocks drawn at random (seed 20261011), 50 per county (Brunswick, New Hanover, Pender), among baseline-reachable blocks; each paired with the engine's nearest ED hospital (min over per-hospital shortest paths, no closures).
- Reference: OSRM car profile route duration (public demo server, after reading its usage terms; <= 1 request/second), origin = block centroid, destination = hospital coordinates. If OSRM is unusable, Valhalla public.
- Reported: MAE (minutes), median absolute percentage error, bias, worst 10 outliers with cause.
- **Pass:** median absolute percentage error <= 20% AND at least 90% of pairs within +-30%.

## Known weaknesses stated up front
Report coordinates are rounded to ~1 km and times are report times, not onset times; reports are positives-only; NWM retro has no surge/tide and no culvert capacity; q2/q10 provenance (OWP "17c") is unverified; the road-name match can fire on cross streets; order-1 reaches are the NWM's weakest. A month-matched control is a weak counterfactual for "an unflooded road" and measures only whether the layer's flow is unusually high when people report flooding.

## Amendment 2 (2026-10-11, BEFORE running T12/T13; only source metadata had been read: HWM list fields/quality categories, raster service description and its generating code)
Run once. Labels: hindcast (NWM v3.0 retro) vs independent observations.

### T12 High-water marks vs road crest
- **HWMs:** USGS STN, event_id **283** ("2018 Florence", confirmed in the live list `https://stn.wim.usgs.gov/STNServices/Events.json`), state NC, from `https://stn.wim.usgs.gov/STNServices/HWMs/FilteredHWMs.json?Event=283&States=NC` (462 rows). Keep `verticalDatumName == NAVD88` and `elev_ft` not null; drop quality "VP: > 0.40 ft" and "Unknown/Historical". Elevation m = elev_ft x 0.3048 (NAVD88). Primary: `hwm_environment == Riverine`; Coastal reported separately (the NWM layer has no surge).
- **Crossings:** statewide seed structures (`data/phase2/t10/structures_nc.parquet`). Long/high-level bridge exclusion: NBI structure length **>= 35 m** (phase-0 provisional cutoff from 14 NGS marks, n = 14, not re-tuned); NCDOT structures take the length of an NBI record within 50 m. Culverts/pipes are kept. Bridges with no length are excluded from the primary set and reported as a sensitivity.
- **Pairing:** each crossing takes its nearest qualifying HWM within **300 m** (primary) / **1 km** (sensitivity). No water-surface-slope correction.
- **Crest:** 3DEP 1 m DEM (NAVD88, metres) from the TNM Access API tile containing the crossing; crest = max DEM sampled every 5 m along the nearest named/ref OSM road (within 30 m) over +-40 m from the crossing (phase-0 `s8` method). No 1 m tile or no road -> not assessed (no fallback DEM).
- **Outcome:** HWM elevation > crest ("water above road crest near the crossing"). Secondary: > crest + 1 m (crest error ~1 m in phase 0).
- **Model:** reach matched within 100 m (q2 >= 1 cfs), NWM v3.0 retro peak flow 2018-09-07..2018-10-07 (the STN event window); peak/q2 and peak/q10.
- **Test:** AUC of peak/q10 separating HWM>crest from HWM<crest crossings; 95% CI by bootstrap over crossings (10,000). **Pass:** lower bound > 0.5. Also: n, share HWM>crest with Wilson CI, per-county counts, same for peak/q2 and for 1 km (secondaries do not change the verdict). If fewer than 5 crossings in either group: reported as "too few to test".

### T13 Observed flood extent
- Sources checked (details in the report): NASA ARIA flood proxy map (Sentinel-1, 2018-09-15, ~30 m): no georeferenced file retrievable (share site is a JS app; NASA Applied Sciences page now redirects; photojournal TIFF is a press image) -> not used. Copernicus EMS: activation for Florence reported, EMSR id/product not found -> not used. USDA NASS inundation layer: cropland/pasture-masked -> not suitable for roads. **Used:** The Nature Conservancy / Schaffer-Smith et al. 2020 Florence flood extent (NC OneMap ImageServer `Florence_Flood_Extent`, KNB doi:10.5063/F1JM280P, CC-BY 4.0), ~20 m (65.6 ft) cells, value 1 = flooded.
- **Image timing (from the published GEE code):** a random-forest classification of Sentinel-1 ascending passes composited (min/max) over **2018-09-18..2018-09-28** (coastal extension to 09-30), plus Landsat post-storm water indices, topography, FATHOM floodplain and land cover; trained on USGS/NCDEMS HWMs and NOAA aerial photos. It is a **multi-date composite, not one snapshot**, and it targets inland flooding, not surge. Because it was trained with HWMs, T12 and T13 are not independent of each other.
- **Crossings:** the 250 hazard-ready seed crossings (pilot). Inside = any flooded cell within **30 m** of the crossing point (primary); sensitivities: cell at the point; within 100 m. Crossings outside the raster footprint: not assessed.
- **Model statistic:** max of flow/q10 (and flow/q2) over **2018-09-18 00:00 .. 2018-09-29 00:00 UTC** (the composite window).
- **Test:** AUC of max flow/q10 separating inside from outside; 95% bootstrap CI over crossings (10,000). **Pass:** lower bound > 0.5. Also shares inside, per county, and for the 147 seed_reliable crossings (secondary). Same "too few" rule (< 5 per group).

## Amendment 3 (2026-10-11, T15 closure-based validation on other storms; written BEFORE the history records were pulled and before any model output was computed). Run once. Hindcast / reanalysis flow vs recorded closures.
What was already known when this was written: phase 0 found the NCDOT TIMS/DriveNC history layer (`NCDOT_TIMSIncidentsHistory`, ArcGIS Online, created 2024-10-15) has 137,980 points / 31,635 lines and only 2 records before 2019; I had seen the layer schema and the most frequent free-text `Reason` strings (e.g. "Road Flooded", "High Water") but no per-month or per-event counts.

### Step 1 - closure records and event selection (data only; counts, no model)
- Records: layer 0 (points, field `StartDateTime`, `EndDateTime`, `Reason`, `Condition`, `EventName`, `EventType`, `EventSubType`, `IsFullClosure`, `CountyName`, lat/lon) and layer 1 (lines, geometry, `Reason`, `IncidentType`, `Condition`, `Closed`, `StartDateUTC`, `EndDateUTC`, `CountyName`). Records per month (all and flood-related) are reported from 2019-01 to the last record.
- **Flood-related closure record** = point record with `IsFullClosure == 'true'` AND (`Reason` or `EventName` or `EventSubType` matches, case-insensitive, the single regex `flood|high water|water over|water across|water on|wash(ed)? ?out|overtop|submerg|under ?water|inundat|erosion`. (Clarification made before any record was pulled: an earlier draft sentence listing `river|creek` is void; only this regex applies.) Records with `EndDateTime` before `StartDateTime` or missing start are dropped. Duplicates (same Id) counted once.
- **Event** = maximal run of flood-related closure records (by start time) with no gap > 72 h. An event is a **candidate** if it has >= 30 records AND >= 3 distinct counties AND starts on/after 2019-01-01. **Chosen events** = all candidates if <= 6, else the 6 with the most records. Selection uses these counts only. Events from before 2023-02-01 use the NWM v3.0 retrospective; later events use the operational analysis (Step 3).
- Event window for flow: [first record start - 48 h, last record start + 24 h].
- If no candidate exists, or the layer does not cover flooding, I report that and stop.

### Step 2 - crossings and labels
- Crossing set: statewide seed structures (`data/phase2/t10/structures_nc.parquet`, NCDOT + NBI, waterway-like) in the counties that have >= 1 flood-related closure record in the event; each matched to the nearest NWM reach <= 100 m with OWP q2/q10 (not assessed: no reach or q2 < 1 cfs; never filled).
- **Positive** = at least one flood-related closure record (point layer, plus the line geometry where the record's Id appears in layer 1) with `StartDateTime` inside the event window lies within **100 m** of the crossing (sensitivity 250 m). **Negative** = every other assessed crossing in the same counties. Caveat fixed in advance: unlisted does not prove open (NCDOT records closures that its staff enter; local roads are under-recorded), so negatives are contaminated with unrecorded closures, which biases the AUC downward.

### Step 3 - flow
- Peak flow over the event window on the crossing's reach; ratios to q2 and q10 (OWP values, the only thresholds). Retro v3.0 via `src/live/nwm_retro.py` for events through 2023-01-31. For later events the operational analysis from `noaa-nwm-pds` (`nwm.YYYYMMDD/analysis_assim/*.channel_rt.tm00.conus.nc`), exact path/coverage/version verified at that time and any model-version mismatch reported (the recurrence thresholds are from a fixed NWM version regardless).

### Step 4 - test (pre-declared)
- Within each event: AUC of peak/q10 for positives vs negatives; 95% CI by cluster bootstrap over counties (10,000). Pooled: mean of event AUCs with a cluster bootstrap over (event, county). **Pass (per event and pooled): lower 95% bound > 0.5.** n positives is reported per event; events or strata with < 5 positives or < 5 negatives are reported as "too few", not dropped from the table.
- Also precision and recall for peak > q2 and peak > q10 (event-level, positives = recorded closures), and strata: stream order (1, 2, >=3), `seed_reliable`-analogue (short bridge/culvert vs long bridge), tidal-like flag (as in phase 0: slope < 2e-4 with order >= 3, or tidal words in the name).
- No change of buffer, windows, regex, thresholds or the pass rule after seeing results; secondaries do not change verdicts.

## Amendment 5 (2026-10-12; T16 multi-feature crossing model; written BEFORE any T16 feature table, fit or score exists). Run once. Earlier amendments and results are not changed.
**What I had seen before writing this:** only the T15 nine-event results (raw peak/q10 AUC, per-event and pooled) and the T15 file schemas. No T16 feature other than those already in the T15 crossing sets has been computed or looked at.
1. **Unit and labels.** One row = crossing x event, exactly the T15 nine-event set (`data/phase2/t15_nine/t15_crossing_sets.parquet`: 34,121 rows, 713 positives at the 100 m rule, negatives = the other assessed crossings in the same counties). Label `pos100`. The 250 m label is not used. Event E5 (window contains 2024-09-26..28, Helene) is **excluded from every training set and from the pooled statistic** and is reported separately (predicted by a model trained on all eight other events).
2. **Features (fixed; computed from the event's flow window, i.e. information that exists only after the peak: this is a hindcast model, not a forecast).**
   (a) `log_r10` = ln(peak flow / q10); (b) `log_r2` = ln(peak flow / q2) (natural log chosen now for both ratios because of the heavy right tail); (c) `hours_above_q2` = number of hourly analysis values in the event's flow window above the reach's q2; (d) structure length in metres (NBI item 49, joined as in `src/validation/52_t15_crossings.py`) with a missing-length indicator; (e) stream order; (f) tidal flag (as T15); (g) structure type (bridge / culvert / pipe / other from the NCDOT structure type); (h) HAND clearance proxy with a missing indicator.
   **Not allowed:** county, population, road class, traffic, any coordinate, event id, date, `reliable_analogue`, anything derived from the closure records.
   **Feature that cannot be built, decided now:** (i) the **Stage IV catchment rain total** needs a basin polygon for each of the ~13,800 crossings' reaches and Stage IV for 2023-2025. The repo holds Stage IV only for 2014-12..2023-01 and basins only for the 236 gauges (`data/derived/stage4_catch`, `data/raw/nldi_basin`); delineating 13,800 basins and fetching 9+ months of Stage IV is outside this pass. It is therefore **not assessed**: dropped, with **no substitute** (no point rain, no county rain), and reported as skipped. (h) HAND: T5 was not run; the only HAND-type values in the repo are the 20-point phase-0 sample (`data/phase0/s4_sample.csv`; none of its crossing ids occurs in the T15 sets) and T12 road-crest elevations for 277 T15 crossings (crest only, no channel elevation, so not a clearance). **Coverage of the HAND clearance proxy over T15 crossings = 0 of 13,769; over rows = 0 of 34,121.** The feature is kept in the specification as an all-missing column and the model is run with and without it; I expect identical results. Missing values are never filled in the data: the tree model reads NaN natively; the logistic model receives the registered missing indicators with the value column set to 0 as a model-input encoding only.
3. **Models (no tuning, nothing chosen on any held-out data).** (A) Logistic regression: `StandardScaler` then `LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000)`, default C = 1, no class weights. (B) **One** `HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_depth=3, max_iter=200, learning_rate=0.1, random_state=0, early_stopping=False)`, no class weights. **The primary model is (B)**; (A) is reported separately and does not change the verdict.
4. **Evaluation.** Leave-one-storm-out over the eight non-Helene events: for each held-out event, fit on the other seven, score the held-out event's rows. Per-event AUC and **pooled AUC = mean of the eight event AUCs** (the T15 statistic). 95% CIs: bootstrap over (event, county) cells of the held-out predictions, 10,000 resamples, same procedure as `src/validation/54_t15_test.py` (the fitted models are held fixed, so these CIs do not include training variance). Baseline = raw peak/q10 AUC on the **same rows and the same resamples**; its T15 nine-event value is 0.690 [0.623, 0.745]. Also reported: within-county AUC (concordant pairs over all pairs, pairs formed only inside an (event, county) cell, cells with < 5 positives or < 5 negatives skipped; CI by cell bootstrap), Helene alone, and the stratum results of the model if time permits. **Permutation importance on held-out events:** for each LOSO fold permute one feature group (5 repeats, fixed seed) in the held-out rows and record the fall in that event's AUC; report the mean over folds. Groups: log_r10; log_r2; hours_above_q2; structure length (+indicator); stream order; tidal; structure type; HAND (+indicator).
5. **Pass rule.** **PASS** iff the primary model's pooled LOSO AUC exceeds the baseline's pooled AUC **and** the 95% CI of the paired bootstrap difference (model minus baseline, same resamples) excludes 0. Logistic, the without-HAND/with-HAND runs and every secondary are descriptive. A pass supports only a statement about ranking crossings by hindcast features within these storms; a fail goes in the failed-tests table.
6. **Leak check (stop rule).** If **any** pooled AUC computed in this task (either model, with or without HAND, baseline excluded) exceeds **0.85**, stop and audit for leakage before reporting anything. The script exits before writing result tables in that case.
7. **No change** of the closure definition, buffers, windows, regex, features, hyperparameters, pass rule or the 0.85 stop rule after seeing results. If a feature fails to build, it is reported as not assessed, not replaced.
